AutoPaginate Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Payment processing

Paid AutoPaginate purchases are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Refund, withdrawal, and cancellation requests for Paddle transactions are handled through Paddle's buyer-support and customer-portal processes, subject to applicable law and Paddle's buyer terms.

2. Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable refund, withdrawal, or consumer-protection rights you may have under applicable law. Where mandatory law provides stronger rights, those rights apply.

3. Technical or product issues

If AutoPaginate has a persistent technical issue or material defect that prevents you from accessing the paid functionality as described, contact us first at feras@enablemygrowth.com so we can attempt to resolve the issue. If the issue cannot be resolved, you may request a refund through Paddle.

4. Annual subscriptions

Canceling Personal Annual prevents future renewal. Unless a refund or statutory withdrawal right applies, cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

5. Lifetime purchases

Personal Lifetime is a one-time digital software purchase. Refund eligibility is determined under applicable consumer law and Paddle's refund/buyer terms.

6. How to request a refund

Use the support or customer-portal link in your Paddle transaction confirmation, or visit Paddle buyer support. You may also contact AutoPaginate support if you need help identifying the relevant transaction.
