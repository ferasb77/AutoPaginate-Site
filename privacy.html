AutoPaginate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how AutoPaginate, a software product of Enable My Growth S.A.R.L., handles information when you use the AutoPaginate Chrome extension and related product services.

2. Captured content

AutoPaginate is designed around a local-first capture workflow. Webpage screenshots, assembled document pages, annotations, redactions, and exported files are processed locally by the extension unless a feature clearly requires an online service. AutoPaginate does not upload captured webpage contents to our servers for advertising or analytics.

3. Account information

The Free tier does not require an account. Personal trial and paid functionality may require Google sign-in. When you sign in, we may process your Supabase user identifier, email address, authentication session information, and entitlement status so the software can determine which features your account may use.

4. Billing information

Paid transactions are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record. We may receive transaction identifiers, customer identifiers, product/price identifiers, subscription status, billing-period information, and entitlement-related metadata. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

5. Optional analytics

Product analytics are opt-in and disabled by default. When enabled, AutoPaginate is designed to exclude captured URLs, page titles, document text, filenames, email addresses, and captured content. Usage values such as page counts or durations may be grouped into coarse ranges.

6. Why we use information

We use account, entitlement, billing, and optional analytics information to provide AutoPaginate, activate trials and paid features, maintain account access, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve the software.

7. Service providers

AutoPaginate may use service providers including Google for authentication, Supabase for authentication and entitlement infrastructure, Paddle for payment processing and billing, and GitHub Pages for this product website. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

8. Retention and security

We retain account and billing-related records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide access, support purchases, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we control.

9. Your choices

You may use the Free tier without signing in. You may keep optional analytics disabled. You may contact us to ask about account information associated with your AutoPaginate account, subject to legal and technical limitations.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to feras@enablemygrowth.com.

AutoPaginate is a software product of Enable My Growth S.A.R.L.
