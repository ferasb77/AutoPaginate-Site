What it does

From web content to a usable document

Built for research, reports, articles, online documents, tenders and reference-heavy webpages.

Capture

Capture visible areas, selected areas, long scrolling webpages, and supported multi-page web documents.

Assemble

Bring captured pages together into a clean document instead of managing a folder full of disconnected screenshots.

Review & export

Review, crop, reorder and annotate captures, then export to PDF or image formats.

Continuous Capture

Keep adding captures from multiple pages or tabs into one working document until you are finished.

Reference Index

Preserve useful hyperlinks already present in captured content and include them as an offline reference archive.

Privacy-focused

Capture and document processing are designed to happen locally. Optional analytics are off by default and exclude captured content.
